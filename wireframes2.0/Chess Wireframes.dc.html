STEP 3 OF 7
next → step 4
Pin the knight to the king
EXPLANATION
Bishop to b5 puts pressure on the knight on c6. Because the king sits behind it, the knight can't move without losing the king — that's a pin.
Try it: drag your bishop from f1 to b5.
COACH
— on your move Bc4
That develops the bishop, but it doesn't create the pin we're after. Try aiming at c6 instead.
